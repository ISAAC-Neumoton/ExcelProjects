## 📌 Executive Summary

Customer acquisition is substantially more capital-intensive than customer retention. Without granular behavioral segmentation, marketing and account management teams often over-discount loyal customers while ignoring at-risk cohorts nearing complete churn.

This project delivers an end-to-end **RFM Segmentation Engine**:
1. Aggregates multi-year raw transaction logs into customer-level metrics using **SQL window functions**.
2. Computes quintile and threshold-based scoring across **Recency**, **Frequency**, and **Monetary Value** to produce granular customer ratings.
3. Translates raw RFM vectors into clear, human-readable lifecycle segments (**Champions**, **Loyal Customers**, **At-Risk**, **Hibernating**, **Lost**).
4. Presents insights through an **interactive Excel dashboard** equipped with dynamic Pivot Tables, metric slicers, and revenue distribution charts.

## 📐 RFM Methodology & Scoring Architecture

Every customer is evaluated across three core operational dimensions using historical transactional data:

$$\text{Customer Record} \longrightarrow \left[ \text{Recency (R)}, \text{Frequency (F)}, \text{Monetary (M)} \right] \longrightarrow \text{Composite Score}$$

### 1. Dimension Definitions

*   **Recency ($R$):** Number of days elapsed between the customer's last completed transaction and the analysis snapshot date. Lower values denote recent engagement.
*   **Frequency ($F$):** Total count of distinct orders or invoices placed by the customer within the evaluation window. Higher values indicate brand loyalty.
*   **Monetary ($M$):** Cumulative net revenue generated by the customer. Higher values identify high-LTV contributors.

---

### 2. Segment Classification Matrix

Composite scores are mapped into clear behavioral categories to prioritize marketing spend and operational outreach:

| Customer Segment | Recency ($R$) | Frequency ($F$) | Monetary ($M$) | Operational Action Plan |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Champions** | High (Top 20%) | High (Top 20%) | High (Top 20%) | Reward loyalty, offer early-access programs, encourage brand ambassadorship. |
| **Loyal Customers** | Moderate–High | High | Moderate–High | Upsell premium services, introduce referral programs. |
| **Potential Loyalists** | High (Recent) | Moderate | Low–Moderate | Implement onboarding automations and product recommendations. |
| **Promising / New** | High (Recent) | Low (1–2 orders) | Low | Send follow-up check-ins and cross-sell related categories. |
| **Needs Attention** | Moderate | Moderate | Moderate | Trigger targeted limited-time discounts or re-engagement surveys. |
| **At-Risk** | Low (Dormant) | Moderate–High | High | High-touch retention outreach, dedicated win-back incentives. |
| **Hibernating** | Low | Low | Low–Moderate | Low-cost re-activation campaigns via email automations[cite: 1]. |
| **Lost / Churned** | Lowest | Lowest | Any | Suppress from paid spend; retain for quarterly survey audits. |
